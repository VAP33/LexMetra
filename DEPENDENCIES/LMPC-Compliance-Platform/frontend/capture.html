Calibrated Package Capture

This does one job well: turn a photo into a real millimetre measurement, by requiring a known-size reference object (a standard ID-1 card — a debit/credit card or driving licence, 85.60mm × 53.98mm) in the same shot as the package. Without this, any "numeral height" number is a guess, and the rule engine will correctly mark it UNCERTAIN rather than PASS or FAIL — see MeasurementMode in the backend.

1. Capture pending

Frame the package's principal display panel AND a reference card together, flat, well-lit, camera parallel to the surface (angle introduces error).

2. Calibrate against reference card pending

Click the two ends of the reference card's LONG edge (85.60mm) on the captured image below.
