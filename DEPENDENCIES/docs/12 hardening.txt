## 4. LEGAL SAFETY IS ABSOLUTE

LexMetra is a legal/compliance inspection platform.

Never weaken these principles:

1. AI extracts evidence.
2. RAG retrieves legal knowledge.
3. Applicability determines scope.
4. Rule engine determines compliance.
5. Human review resolves material uncertainty.

Never:

* hallucinate legal rules
* hallucinate evidence
* treat missing evidence as failure
* treat OCR failure as legal failure
* treat missing RAG as PASS
* treat missing VLM as PASS
* treat barcode presence as compliance
* treat pHash as counterfeit proof
* treat uncalibrated measurements as verified
* treat an uncited source as verified law
* treat absence of evidence as proof of violation

Most importantly:

`NOT_OBSERVED != FAIL`

`CONFLICT != CONFIDENT FACT`

`NO_RAG != PASS`

`NO_VLM != PASS`

`NO_EVIDENCE != PROVEN_VIOLATION`
