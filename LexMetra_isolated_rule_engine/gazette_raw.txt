(Department of Consumer Affairs)
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 23rd October, 2025.
G.S.R. . 778(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by (j) and (q) of sub-section (2) of section 52 of the
Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend
the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, namely:-
1. Short title and commencement. – (1) These rules may be called the Legal Metrology (Packaged
Commodities) Amendment Rules, 2025.
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the official gazette.
2. In the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (hereinafter referred to as the principal rules), in
rule 2, to clause (h), the following proviso shall be inserted, namely:-
―Provided that for packages containing medical devices, the provisions of the Medical Devices Rules, 2017,
shall apply to make declarations;‖.
3. In the principal rules, in rule 7,-
(i) in sub-rule (2), the following proviso shall be inserted at the end, namely:-
―Provided that for packages containing medical devices, the provisions of the Medical Devices Rules, 2017,
shall apply for the height of any numeral and letter to make declarations.‖;
(ii) to sub-rule (3), the following proviso shall be inserted at the end, namely:-
―Provided that for packages containing medical devices, the provisions of the Medical Devices Rules, 2017,
shall apply for the width of any numeral and letter to make declarations.‖.
