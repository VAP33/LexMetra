Statutory Verification Principle: Legal Metrology Rule 7(2) mandates exact minimum numeral heights (e.g. 1.0mm to 6.0mm) based on Principal Display Panel (PDP) area. This tool uses an ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 standard card (85.60 mm) placed in-frame to convert camera pixels into certified SI millimetres, elevating OCR estimations into defensible statutory evidence.
